Draw a simple and big organic form. Reinforce it with contour lines. Add another organic form imagining it dangling on top of the first form. Repeat this process a couple of times. Finally, add line weight and cast shadows. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/organicintersections).

# Goal

The purpose of this exercise is to develop the understanding of 3d space and how form exists within it in relation to each other, in a different manner from the Forms Intersections exercise.
