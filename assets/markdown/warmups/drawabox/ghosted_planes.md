Place four dots on the page. Using the ghosting method, connect the four dots to form a plane. Also construct lines between the corners, and bisect the plane in both dimensions. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/ghostedplanes).

# Goal

The goal of this exercise is the same as the ghosted lines exercise, but for more complex shapes, to get used to slowing down and thinking about units of work, which are lines.
