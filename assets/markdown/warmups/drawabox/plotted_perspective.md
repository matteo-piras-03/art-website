Divide the page in 3 rectangles using a ruler, Then, on each frame, draw an horizon line and two vanishing points to the sides.
Draw a vertical edge, plot its corners back to the vanishing points, place two outer vertical edges, plot them back to the vanishing points, and connect the final two corners for the back vertical edge.
Repeat this process 5-6 times per frame. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/plottedperspective).

# Goal

The goal of this exercise is to introduce students to the idea of working with vanishing points, and also getting used to have a lot of lines on the page.
