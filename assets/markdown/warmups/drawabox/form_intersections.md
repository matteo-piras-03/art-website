Draw a series of solid forms (boxes, cylinders, spheres, cones and pyramids) roughly equilateral and with shallow convergence, making sure they overlap on one another, forming a big group. Inside the overlap, define the form intersections. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/formintersections).

# Goal

The purpose of this exercise is to develop the understanding of 3d space and how form exists within it in relation to each other.
