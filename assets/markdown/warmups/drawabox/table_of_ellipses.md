Divide the page into a table with two columns and a bunch of rows. Using the ghosting method, draw ellipses of various degrees within each cell. Optionally, you can also draw a wave through the section, and filling the space between the wave and the table. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/tablesofellipses).

# Goal

The goal of this exercise is to practice drawing ellipses in a variety of sizes, orientations and degrees. It also sets out a clear space each ellipse is meant to occupy, giving us a means to assess wether or not an ellipse was successful or not.
