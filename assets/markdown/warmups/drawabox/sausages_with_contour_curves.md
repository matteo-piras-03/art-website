Draw a sausage form, consisting of two spheres connected by a consistent central tube (don't draw these forms explicitely). Draw a minor axis line through the sausage. Then, you can add a few contour ellipses, varying in degrees, or you can add contour lines instead. Finally, add an ellipse on the tip of the sausage. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/contourlines).

# Goal

This exercise shows us that contour lines reinforce the illusion of three-dimensionality of a form and gives us a better understanding on how these forms exist in 3d space.
