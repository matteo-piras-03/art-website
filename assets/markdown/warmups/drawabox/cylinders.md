Placing minor axes, draw a series of cylinders with either rapid or shallow convergence. For construction inside a box, draw a box first, then the minor axis and the ellipses in planes. For detailed instructions, click [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/250cylinders).

# Goal

The goal of this exercise is to get used to drawing freely rotated cylinders without explicit vanishing points, and to draw cylinders inside boxes to reinforce the understanding of 3D space.
